Our Story

From Anna Incubator to Sriram Research Park

Sriram Research Park, formerly known as Anna Incubator, is a startup incubation centre under the Sriram Educational Trust, established in 1993. Located at the AC Tech Campus of Anna University, Guindy, Chennai, we provide aspiring entrepreneurs with the mentorship, infrastructure, funding access, and network they need to transform bold ideas into successful ventures.

Our parent institution, Sriram Engineering College, has produced 9,430+ engineers and boasts 15,000+ alumni worldwide with a 90% placement rate. This rich academic ecosystem fuels our incubation programs with deep technical talent and research capabilities.

Chairman's Message

“

At Sriram Research Park, we believe that every great startup begins with a bold idea and the right ecosystem to nurture it. Over the years, we have had the privilege of witnessing extraordinary transformations — from nascent ideas to funded ventures making real-world impact. Startups like Raptee Energy raising ₹80 Crore, MEINE Electric shaping the future of electric mobility, and Reneonix pioneering circular economy solutions are testaments to what happens when talent meets opportunity.

Our commitment extends beyond funding. We provide hands-on mentorship from industry veterans, access to cutting-edge labs, legal support, and a vibrant community of innovators. Together, we are building the next generation of Indian innovators who will solve critical national and global challenges.

Dr. R. Narayanasamy

Chairman, Sriram Educational Trust
